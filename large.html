US Large-Cap Screener — Growth Below the 200-DMA

The same screen as the mega-cap dashboard, run over the 50 largest US large-caps (roughly $100B–$200B market cap) that sit just below mega-cap size — no overlap with the mega-cap list. It computes the top 10 — not a fixed list — that pass a two-part gate: (1) revenue & net-income growth in USD above your minimum rates, and (2) price trading below its 200-day average by at least the amount you set. Prices refresh live on load and every 2 minutes; the % vs 200-DMA, market cap and P/E recompute from that live price. Click any name for its chart, technicals and financials.

How the screen works

Growth in USD Below the 200-DMA Gold panel
  • Filter 1 — growth. Revenue and net income must each compound above the minimum per-annum rate you choose (all in USD).
  • Filter 2 — entry. Price must sit at least X% below its 200-day average — on a pullback vs its long-term trend, rather than extended above it.
  • Ranked by growth (revenue + net-income CAGR). Move the sliders and the top 10 rebuilds instantly.
  • Universe: 50 US-listed large-caps across telecom, healthcare, industrials, energy, financials, retail, semis and software. Only companies reporting in USD are included, so growth is directly comparable.
